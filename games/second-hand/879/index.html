Night Security Log — A. Bell

11/14/1993 — Fire in Unit 41 tonight. Everyone got out. Almost everyone. I keep hearing a small voice on the speakers.
1993 年 11 月 14 日——今晚 41 号铺起火了。大家都逃出来了。几乎所有人。我总是在喇叭里听见一个小小的声音。
12/03/1993 — The doctors say I should rest. But someone has to do the night shift. Someone has to keep the lights on.
1993 年 12 月 3 日——医生说我该休息。可总得有人值夜班。总得有人让灯一直亮着。
06/18/2001 — I play music in the empty halls at night. It feels less lonely. For me, and for them.
2001 年 6 月 18 日——夜里我在空荡荡的走廊里放音乐。这样就没那么孤单了。对我，对他们，都是。
11/30/2009 — They're closing us down. When the lights go out, who will keep them company? I'll read their names every Thursday. So no one is forgotten.
2009 年 11 月 30 日——他们要关掉这里了。灯一灭，谁来陪着他们？我会每周四念一遍他们的名字。好让谁都不会被忘记。
12/17/2009 — A new guest tonight. A little boy who loves tapes. Theodore. I said his name very carefully.
2009 年 12 月 17 日——今晚来了一位新客人。一个喜欢磁带的小男孩。Theodore。我很小心地念了他的名字。
